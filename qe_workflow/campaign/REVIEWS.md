Most factual claims about the report hold, usually with a caveat the proposal leaves out:
- **Contacts:** TCAD uses ideal Ohmic Pd contacts.
- **Pd barrier:** a confinement-raised Pd/IWO barrier at 2 nm is plausible but unverified.
- **Off-state floors:** they rise strongly with thickness and their origin is undetermined. Gate leakage is rejected only at 6.3 and 13.2 nm, and thermal generation is rejected.
- **Mobility and threshold:** the measured μFE values are 12.15, 10.95 and 50.17 cm²/Vs, and the threshold drops from 0.644 to 0.083 V.
- **Mobility step:** contacts, roughness, electrostatics and tail filling cannot cause the ×4.6 step. This is strongly supported but model-conditional, and contacts were never tested in ATLAS.
- **Confinement:** it is rejected as the cause of the 2 ≈ 6.3 ≫ 13.2 pattern.
- **Working hypothesis:** a structural transition, plausible and unverified.
- **Inventory:** 11 structures are built; the relaxed 2 nm slab and the 2 nm IWO slab are pending.
- **Atom counts:** about 500 atoms at 6 nm and more than 1000 at 13 nm (computed: 496 and 1056).

**One premise is refuted.** Nothing in the package shows that a change of contact potential brought the simulated off-current toward the measurement. The floors are explicitly not modelled; the simulated off-state is a numerical zero (≤6.3e-18 A/µm). No run changes a contact work function, and the older Schottky deck was never run. The ideal Ohmic contact is already the zero-barrier limit, so any DFT Φ_Bn placed in ATLAS can only lower the simulated current. The loop "DFT Φ_Bn → TCAD → I_off → compare" therefore cannot work in V1. It would also need launches beyond the exhausted 57/57 budget and a new timestamped register.

Block 2's floor mechanism (V_O lowering the barrier, trap-assisted tunnelling) appears nowhere in the package. An older document lists "contact injection" among undetermined floor candidates, but the later analysis favours an ungated IWO path. The floors are gate-independent, and that is hard to reconcile with a contact in series with a depleted channel.

**Block 3's 6.3/13.2 nm slabs are poor value.** The report already bounds confinement there at the THEORY level: ≤0.05 eV at 6.3 nm and ~0.01 eV at 13.2 nm, and the m*(t) law changes the Drude mobility by only ~3% between them (computed). Cost estimates:
- 6.3 nm: about 185 GB per vc-relax and 4.5–10.5 h per BFGS step.
- 13.2 nm: about 740 GB and 20–90 h per step.
- 2 nm HSE: exact-exchange arrays of about 110 GB.
All three exceed one 94 GB H100 NVL. A ~3 nm (256-atom) slab is the better bridge point, because the V1 law crosses the effective-mass bound at 2.98 nm. Note that the old 3 nm template in qe_workflow breaks the electron-count rule and must be rebuilt.

**The proposal misses several targets the package names explicitly:**
- **Tail-energy reference:** whether tail states follow the confined or the bulk band edge, and how localized they are. The report calls this "the dominant model-form uncertainty at 2 nm".
- **Back surface:** the air-exposed surface with adsorbates, versus the Al2O3 front. Every DFT slab so far is H-terminated on both sides.
- **Deep states:** the model underestimates states near Ec−0.15 to −0.2 eV by ×2.2–2.8. The W-5d level at ~0.19 eV below the host CB (1 nm, PBE, preliminary) is a candidate cause to test.
- **Pd barrier criterion:** the barrier must be ≥0.20 eV, which needs a CNL margin of ≤0.15 eV or dEc(2 nm) ≥0.6 eV. A pre-registered DFT test fits here. The provisional 2 nm ΔEg is ≈0.33 eV, which tightens the condition to a CNL margin of ≤~0.13–0.17 eV (computed).
- **Unresolved prerequisites:**
  - The doped-slab vacuum shift of 1.1–1.6 eV is unexplained.
  - The W magnetism criterion is not met.
  - The 1 nm IWO effective mass is missing.
- **Degenerate limit of defect cells:** one defect per 10.34 Å cell is 1e14 cm⁻²-scale, so the slab E_F is not the device E_F.
- **Thickness definition:** ±0.3 nm at 2 nm moves E1 by about ±25%.
- **Non-identifiability:** χ, dipole, Q_f and N_D all enter one rigid offset per ID–VG curve. DFT values for them cannot be validated without C–V, optical-gap, ID–VD or TLM data.
- **6.3 nm anomaly:** it is not DFT-addressable. Its candidates are device spread or sweep history, and a density-dependent mobility.
- **Target paper:** Januar 2026 already makes DFT claims, including W passivating V_O and an IWO/HfO2 interface model. Block 1 #9 and #14 would test these directly.

**Budget (estimate).** Blocks 1–2 as written imply roughly 2–8k GPU-h, against 85–90 GPU-h used so far. The amorphous AIMD items alone are about 400–2800 GPU-h per structure.

**Report inconsistency.** Chapter 11 line 77 still says a 52-launch budget is exhausted and that the TMUN and overlap checks were not done. Runs 0054 and 0055 did both, and solver.yaml now allows 57 launches.

## PHYSICS review

**Summary.** Review of the proposed DFT campaign through the physics-and-method lens. PKG is C:\Users\moham\Downloads\IWO_Local_Codex_Handoff (1)\IWO_PHYSICS_CONSTRAINED_MODEL_V1. I only read files; nothing was run or changed.

The proposal uses the right DFT vocabulary. But two of its main premises are contradicted by the package itself.

1. **Contacts do not explain the off-state floors.** The idea that a Pd contact barrier, or oxygen vacancies (V_O) near Pd, explain the floors does not hold, for three reasons:
   - The floors do not change with gate voltage. A barrier in series with a gate-depleted channel can only lower the current.
   - The earlier Astra model already used a Schottky Pd contact (work function 4.45 eV plus SURF.REC). It still gave native zeros in the off state. Changing the emission coefficient changed the on-current by only 0.38 % and left all 54 zero points unchanged.
   - The floor ratios between thicknesses (x33 and x43) cannot follow an exp(-dPhi/kT) law driven by confinement.

2. **DFT at 6.3 and 13.2 nm is not worth running.**
   - The expected confinement difference there is 0.02-0.05 eV. This is no larger than the DFT noise.
   - A simple effective-mass estimate already gives it.
   - Consistent relaxed cells (about 500 and about 1056 atoms) are estimated at 250 GB to over 1 TB of memory. That is beyond one H100.
   - Crystalline slabs cannot address the x4.6 mobility step in any case.

The useful core is smaller than proposed:
- finish the 2 nm slab;
- split the gap opening between conduction and valence band. The hybrid-functional (HSE) part of this can be read from outputs that already exist: in bulk, HSE minus PBE gives about -1.01 eV on the valence-band edge and +0.12 to +0.17 eV on the conduction-band edge. At 1 nm, HSE adds about 0.06 eV to the conduction-band shift (computed here, not validated);
- resolve the unexplained 1.1-1.65 eV vacuum-level shift of the W-doped 1 nm slab before any doped-slab alignment is trusted;
- test W-5d localization in bulk, with dilution and with HSE;
- compute bulk V_O formation energies and charge transition levels with HSE and finite-size corrections, reported over the full oxygen chemical-potential range because the growth conditions are unknown;
- add a 3 nm slab instead of 6.3 and 13.2 nm;
- treat the Pd contact in stages, starting with a cheap bulk branch-point estimate.

The amorphous models, the Al2O3 interface models, the full stack and most of the Pd/W/V_O interface jobs should be dropped or deferred. They are unaffordable, or they cannot be identified from one transfer curve per film, or both.
