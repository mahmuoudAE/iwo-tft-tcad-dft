\section{First-principles calculations}
\label{sec:mono-res-dft}

\paragraph{Recorded totals.} Up to 4 October 2026: about 8\,300 weighted CPU core-hours and 85--90 GPU-hours at CERN, of
which about 3\,000 core-hours and 50 GPU-hours were lost to failures, and about 190 core-hours on a laptop
(\cref{sec:dft-resources}). The jobs after that date (the 2~nm W film, the sub-nanometre films, the hybrid checks, the
vacancy cells and the amorphous campaign) are not totalled in the public record; the amorphous total is a pending item
of the campaign (\pendingph{CPU and GPU hours after 4 October}).

\paragraph{Memory.} The 176-atom 2~nm film needs about 66--82~GB for its SCF (\cref{sec:mono-dft-cost}); the 2.0~nm
amorphous chains used 85.6--90.2~GB of the 95.8~GB of one GPU on the single-GPU route, which required split species
labels. The default exact-exchange cutoff ran out of memory for the hybrid 1~nm film, which is why 160~Ry was used.

\paragraph{Infrastructure.} CERN HTCondor, submitted from lxplus, data on EOS. A snapshot of the pool on 6 October
2026 listed 405 GPUs on 79 nodes (144 H100 NVL, 96 H200, 72 A100-40GB and older cards); in the amorphous campaign the
H100/H200 pool was saturated and two-GPU requests matched no slot. Observed throughput was about 0.8 GPU starts per hour.
Kerberos tickets of running jobs are not renewed, so long jobs needed separate credential handling.

\section{Energy (estimate)}
\label{sec:mono-res-energy}

Assuming 0.4~kW per GPU (the H100 NVL power limit) and 10~W per CPU core including node overheads (\prov{assumed}
round numbers, not measurements), the recorded first-principles usage up to 4 October corresponds to about
$8\,300\times0.010 + 90\times0.4 \approx 120$~kWh, of the order of a week of a household's electricity use; the device
simulations are negligible by comparison. The amorphous campaign, with relaxations of 250--300-atom cells running for days
on single GPUs, is likely larger, but its usage is not recorded publicly. These numbers are given to make the
computational cost of the study concrete, not as an energy audit.
