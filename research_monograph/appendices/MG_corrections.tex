\chapter{Corrections and supersession register}
\label{app:mono-corrections}

A result that was once reported and later corrected is kept, together with its correction, so that a reader of an
older document can tell what changed and why. This register lists every correction that bears on the monograph. The
identifiers X1--X8 are cited in the text as \corr{X1} etc. A correction never deletes the original statement: the
original lines remain in the dated logs and in git history (\file{RESULTS_LOG.md} keeps every superseded line
unchanged and adds dated errata).

\section{Rules applied}
\begin{enumerate}
\item A later source does not win because it is later. A correction is accepted only when it names the error and the
evidence for the new statement (a raw-output line, a recomputation, or a protocol). Every entry below has one.
\item Corrections of numbers are checked against the raw outputs where the monograph has them
(\cref{app:mono-registers}); corrections of wording are checked against the label of the claim in the ledger.
\item Where the monograph re-checked a proposed correction and found it wrong, the rejection is recorded too
(entry R1).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Inconsistencies found in the record (not corrected here)}
The following inconsistencies were found in frozen or private record files. The monograph does not edit those files;
it states which reading it uses. They are listed for the record keepers.

\begin{enumerate}
\item The register header lists the post-freeze amendments as ``A17 to A31, A34 and A35'' in one place, while A36--A44
exist. The monograph uses the amendment texts themselves.
\item A36 time: 2026-10-12T13:21Z in the register (commit b164faa) against 13:25Z in the decision log (D-40). The
monograph uses the register time.
\item The register still marks A15 as ``proposed'' in one passage, although D-16 and D-26 approved it.
\item Time zones: decision-log dates are local (UTC+8), register times are UTC; a freeze at 2026-10-10T18:33Z appears
as a decision of 2026-10-11.
\item The SRH bound is stated as ``$\geq10^{14}$ below the currents'' and as $10^{-28}$ against $\geq10^{-15}$~A/\textmu m
(a factor $10^{13}$) in two places of the campaign design. The monograph quotes the numbers, not the factor.
\item The bulk amorphous references are said to have ``125 atoms each''; by the composition rules aIWO\_bulk\_s1
(In$_{49}$W$_1$O$_{76}$) has 126.
\item One plan names MACE-MP-0, while the validation protocol, the register and the ledger use MACE-MPA-0. The
monograph follows the validation protocol.
\item Stage counts of the campaign: 181 + 19 reserve (design table) against 184 entries + 16 reserve (regenerated matrix).
\item \file{01_CURRENT_STATE.md} (2026-10-07) is stale for DFT, the campaign and the budget; it is used only for device and
data facts.
\end{enumerate}
