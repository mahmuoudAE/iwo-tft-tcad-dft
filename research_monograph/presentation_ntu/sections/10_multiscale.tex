\begin{frame}[t]{Four measurements can separate what transfer curves cannot}

  \begin{itemize}

      per film \lab{derived}\tenC{C018}; only one $\Delta V_0$ shared by three films (C10) lets the computed inputs fail.
  \end{itemize}
