\begin{frame}[t]{The 176-atom film needs 66--82\,GB; memory set where it ran}
  \thirteenTop\thirteendense
  \begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.54\textwidth}
      \thirteenHead{Plane-wave cost for a cell of $N$ atoms}
      {\centering$t_{\mathrm{it}} \propto \thirteenNb^{2}\,\thirteenNpw \propto N^{3}, \qquad
         M_{\psi} \propto \thirteenNb\,\thirteenNpw\,\thirteenNk \propto N^{2}\thirteenNk$\par}
      \vspace{0.8mm}
      {\scriptsize\color{inkgrey} $t_{\mathrm{it}}$: time per SCF iteration; $M_{\psi}$: wave-function memory;
        $\thirteenNb$, $\thirteenNpw$, $\thirteenNk$: bands, plane waves, $k$ points. Linear-scaling
        methods\sref{Goedecker1999} not used.\par}
      \vspace{1mm}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item 1\,nm $\to$ 2\,nm film: bands 328 $\to$ 680, plane waves $+29$\,\%; wave functions $\times2.7$,
          $\thirteenNb^{2}\thirteenNpw$ work $\times5.5$ \lab{derived}.
        \item 2\,nm film: $680\times327\,585\times16$\,B $=3.6$\,GB per $k$ point; \texttt{pw.x} estimates
          82\,GB per process; the final SCF needed 66\,GB. Hence a large-memory GPU or a distributed CPU run.
        \item HSE06, 1\,nm film: the default exact-exchange cutoff ran out of memory; 160\,Ry was used.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
    \begin{column}{0.42\textwidth}
      \thirteenHead{CERN HTCondor GPU pool, 6 Oct 2026}
      {\footnotesize
      \begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
        \toprule
        GPU model & GPUs \\
        \midrule
        H100 NVL & 144 \\
        H200 & 96 \\
        A100-PCIE-40GB & 72 \\
        Older (V100, V100S, T4) & 93 \\
        \midrule
        Total, on 79 nodes & 405 \\
        \bottomrule
      \end{tabular}\par}
      \src{Pool snapshot (\texttt{resources.json}); jobs submitted from lxplus, data on EOS.}
      \vspace{1mm}
      \begin{itemize}
        \item One 96-atom SCF: 1\,h 48\,min on 16 CPU cores, 9\,min on one H100 NVL \lab{measured}.
        \item About 0.8 GPU starts per hour; two-GPU requests of the amorphous campaign matched no slot.
      \end{itemize}
    \end{column}
  \end{columns}
  \takeaway{For the large cells, memory and access to large GPUs, not core-hours, were the limits.}
\end{frame}
