\section{Demonstrated, or computed within a stated scope}
\label{sec:concl-demonstrated}

Measured and derived device facts, falsified predictions and results that follow from frozen rules are demonstrated.
First-principles results are listed here as computed within their scope (crystalline cells, PBE, the stated settings);
they are not measurements of the device films.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{The measured devices.} The thresholds (0.662, 0.644, 0.083~V), off-state floors and on-currents of the
2.0, 6.3 and 13.2~nm devices follow from the curves under the registered protocol and are reproduced independently
within rounding; the thickness dependence is non-uniform, and the 31.8~nm device does not switch off
(\claim{C002}, \claim{C003}, \claim{C026}, \claim{C029}; one device per film).
\item \emph{The device models are calibrations.} Every per-film model reproduces its own curve by construction, and every
out-of-sample threshold prediction for 6.3~nm failed ($-0.29$, $-0.37$, $-0.23$~V; \claim{C014}, \prov{falsified}).
\item \emph{Crystalline confinement (computed).} PBE gap openings of $+2.25$, $+1.31$, $+0.90$ and $+0.335$~eV at H--H
thicknesses of 0.461, 0.695, 0.930 and 1.962~nm; at 1 and 2~nm they agree within 0.04 and 0.005~eV with an independent
published calculation \citep{Lin2022}; at 2~nm 84\,\% of the opening is in the conduction band; the hybrid-functional
ratio is 1.06--1.11 at 1~nm (\claim{C020}, \claim{C021}, \claim{C039}). The 0.461~nm film is In$_8$(OH)$_{24}$ and the
0.695~nm film is polar.
\item \emph{Masses (computed).} PBE band-edge electron mass $0.154\,m_0$ in bulk, rising to $0.203$ and $0.279\,m_0$ at
1.96 and 0.93~nm; no meaningful single hole mass (\claim{C039}, \claim{C040}). The bulk Kane relation reproduces the
2~nm film mass within 1\,\% and overestimates the 1~nm mass by 10\,\%, without fitting (\cref{sec:mono-conf-test},
\prov{derived}).
\item \emph{W in crystalline cells, at the computed filling (computed).} W occupies the 24d site; its W-5d-rich level is a
resonance 1.2--1.8~eV above the host band bottom, near $E_F$; the earlier deep-donor reading was a band-count artefact
(\claim{C022}, \corr{X1}).
\item \emph{The machine-learned amorphous structures failed their registered force check} in the W first shell
(\claim{C037}).
\item \emph{The registered H-N outcome cannot be a crossover verdict} (\cref{prop:mono-hn}; follows from the frozen
rules).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Strongly supported}
\label{sec:concl-supported}

\begin{enumerate}
\item A thickness-independent series resistance cannot create the thickness trend (it would need 83\,\% of $\Vd$ at
2~nm; \claim{C019}, \prov{derived}).
\item The local swing of the three thinner films collapses against $\Id/\Ion$ in data and simulation (\claim{C007},
\prov{observed}).
\item The published mobilities are reproduced numerically, with no free factor, by the peak of the saturation formula
applied to the same curves (\claim{C030}, \prov{derived}); the formula does not describe these curves as a mobility
(\cref{sec:mono-prov-mobility}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Inferred, derived under stated conditions, or computed but unresolved}
\label{sec:concl-inferred}

\begin{enumerate}
\item The off-state floor is unlikely to be gate leakage: it is flat in $\Vg$, but no gate current was recorded and the
registered tests are sealed (\claim{C005}, \prov{inferred}).
\item The saturation formula is the leading candidate for the paper's mobility definition (\claim{C031},
\prov{inferred}; \claim{C024} stays \prov{unresolved} until the fabrication group confirms its recipe).
\item \emph{Confinement and the threshold step.} Transferred rigidly, the crystalline PBE conduction-band law gives
44\,\% of the measured 2.0--13.2~nm threshold difference (monograph arithmetic, \prov{derived}); in the earlier report's
calibrated model the realised confinement term was about 50\,\% of that step; and the register's audit forecasts an
all-inputs-off counterfactual share of about 18\,\% (\amend{A13}; a model counterfactual, not an identified physical
contribution). The threshold data cannot distinguish readings with and without confinement (\claim{C018}). That
confinement does not account for the step by itself is therefore \prov{inferred}, for crystalline inputs applied to
amorphous or nanocrystalline films.
\item \emph{The 6.3~nm failures.} Every failed 6.3~nm prediction is related to the measured threshold staying flat
between 2.0 and 6.3~nm, where the computed confinement implies a 0.2~V change (\cref{sec:mono-ms-explain},
\prov{derived}); the arithmetic describes the failures and assigns no cause.
\item \emph{Oxygen vacancies near W (computed, unresolved).} In degenerately doped crystalline \InO{}, a vacancy far
from W costs 0.73~eV more than in pure \InO{} (passes its registered threshold), and a vacancy next to W a further
0.53--0.56~eV (significance unresolved); the spin sensitivity is unassessed, H-L is \prov{unresolved} overall, and
neither difference is a formation energy at device conditions (\claim{C038}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Required validation}
\label{sec:mono-concl-validation}

\paragraph{Measurements (by information per cost).}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Off-state floor against drain bias on the existing chips (strip $\times7$ against diffusion $\times1.02$ between 0.1
and 0.7~V), with the gate current recorded.
\item Device statistics: at least ten devices per thickness from one fabrication run, with dual sweeps and recorded sweep
history.
\item Thickness metrology of the same films (XRR, TEM) and the stack order.
\item Capacitance--voltage flat band, optical gap and Hall density against thickness, for \InO{} and IWO.
\item A dense thickness series (about 1~nm steps, 3--15~nm) at 0, 2 and 4\,\% W, capped and uncapped.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Calculations.} A dipole-corrected SCF of the 0.695~nm film; a lower-density and a hybrid-functional W cell;
charged vacancy cells with corrections; band runs of the W films; and, for the amorphous question, a re-registered
series with at least two independently quenched seeds per endpoint relaxed in one smearing family. Costs are in
\cref{tab:mono-mass-runs}.

\paragraph{Governance.} The meeting with the fabrication group on permission, attribution, AI processing and authorship;
an external timestamp for the frozen registrations; adoption of the charter; the release decision for the sealed
amorphous results; and completion of the device campaign under its registered rules.

\section{Outlook}
\label{sec:mono-concl-outlook}

The strongest original result available today is computational and crystalline: a confinement series of \InO{} films
whose mass increase is consistent with the bulk band structure, with an explicit account of what transfers to a device model
and what does not. The central dimensional question about W in amorphous films is protocol-ready but, by the frozen
rules, cannot be answered as registered by the present campaign. A manuscript framework for \emph{npj Computational
Materials} that states these limits is in \file{research_monograph/manuscript_npj/}; it contains placeholders for every
sealed result and makes no claim that the evidence does not support.

\begin{keyresult}
Demonstrated: the device metrics, the failure of every 6.3~nm prediction, the failed ML force check and the
impossibility of a registered H-N crossover verdict. Computed within scope (crystalline, PBE): the confinement series,
with masses consistent with bulk non-parabolicity (1\,\% at 2~nm, 10\,\% at 1~nm), and the resonant W level at high
filling (deep-donor reading withdrawn). Strongly supported: series resistance cannot explain the trend; the swing
collapses against $\Id/\Ion$; the published mobilities are reproduced numerically by the saturation-formula peak.
Inferred or unresolved: the floor is unlikely to be gate leakage; confinement alone does not account for the threshold
step; every 6.3~nm failure is related to a flat threshold where confinement implies 0.2~V (no cause assigned); H-L is
unresolved. Everything about amorphous W and the shared
model's transfer is sealed. The decisive next steps are measurements: the floor's drain-bias dependence, device
statistics and thickness metrology.
\end{keyresult}
