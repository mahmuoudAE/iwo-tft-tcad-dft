\section{Demonstrated}
\label{sec:concl-demonstrated}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{The measured devices.} The thresholds (0.662, 0.644, 0.083~V), off-state floors and on-currents of the
2.0, 6.3 and 13.2~nm devices follow from the curves under the registered protocol and are reproduced independently
within rounding; the thickness dependence is non-uniform, and the 31.8~nm device does not switch off
(\claim{C002}, \claim{C003}, \claim{C026}, \claim{C029}; one device per film).
\item \emph{The device models are calibrations.} Every per-film model reproduces its own curve by construction, and every
out-of-sample threshold prediction for 6.3~nm failed ($-0.29$, $-0.37$, $-0.23$~V; \claim{C014}, \prov{falsified}).
\item \emph{Crystalline confinement.} PBE gap openings of $+2.25$, $+1.31$, $+0.90$ and $+0.335$~eV at H--H thicknesses
of 0.461, 0.695, 0.930 and 1.962~nm, reproduced against an independent published calculation at 1 and 2~nm; at 2~nm
84\,\% of the opening is in the conduction band; the hybrid-functional ratio is 1.06--1.11 at 1~nm (\claim{C020},
\claim{C021}, \claim{C039}). The 0.461~nm film is In$_8$(OH)$_{24}$ and the 0.695~nm film is polar.
\item \emph{Masses.} PBE band-edge electron mass $0.154\,m_0$ in bulk, rising to $0.203$ and $0.279\,m_0$ at 1.96 and
0.93~nm; no meaningful single hole mass (\claim{C039}, \claim{C040}). The rise of the film mass is reproduced within
2--10\,\% by the bulk Kane relation without fitting (\cref{sec:mono-conf-test}, \prov{derived}).
\item \emph{W in crystalline cells, at the computed filling.} W occupies the 24d site; its W-5d-rich level is a
resonance 1.2--1.8~eV above the host band bottom, near $E_F$; the earlier deep-donor reading was a band-count artefact
(\claim{C022}, \corr{X1}).
\item \emph{The machine-learned amorphous structures failed their registered force check} in the W first shell
(\claim{C037}).
\item \emph{The registered H-N outcome cannot be a crossover verdict} (\cref{prop:mono-hn}).
\item \emph{Confinement does not account for the threshold step by itself.} Transferred rigidly, the computed
conduction-band shift accounts for 44\,\% of the 2.0--13.2~nm threshold difference; in the earlier report's analysis the
realised confinement accounted for about 50\,\%, and the threshold data cannot distinguish readings with and without
confinement (\claim{C018}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Strongly supported}
\label{sec:concl-supported}

\begin{enumerate}
\item A thickness-independent series resistance cannot create the thickness trend (it would need 83\,\% of $\Vd$ at
2~nm; \claim{C019}).
\item The off-state floor is not gate leakage (flat in $\Vg$; \claim{C005}, \prov{inferred}; no gate current recorded).
\item The published mobilities are reproduced, with no free factor, by the peak of the saturation formula applied to the
same curves, which makes that formula the leading candidate for the paper's definition (\claim{C030}, \claim{C031}); the
formula does not describe these curves as a mobility (\cref{sec:mono-prov-mobility}).
\item The local swing of the three thinner films collapses against $\Id/\Ion$ in data and simulation (\claim{C007},
\prov{observed}).
\item The 6.3~nm failures follow from the measured threshold staying flat between 2.0 and 6.3~nm, where the computed
confinement implies a 0.2~V change (\cref{sec:mono-ms-explain}, \prov{derived}).
\item W raises the energy of a neutral oxygen vacancy in degenerately doped crystalline \InO{} (0.73~eV far from W;
\claim{C038}; H-L overall unresolved, not a formation energy at device conditions).
\end{enumerate}

\section{Rejected or withdrawn}
\label{sec:mono-concl-rejected}

\begin{itemize}
\item Prediction of the 6.3~nm threshold with shared parameters (\claim{C014}, \prov{falsified}).
\item W as a deep, localised donor in the 1~nm crystalline film (\corr{X1}, withdrawn).
\item The V1 package as ``publication-ready''; run\_0014 as a validation (\corr{X5}).
\item Agreement of the 13.2~nm swing (holds only after an assumed floor subtraction; \claim{C010}).
\item Machine-learned structures as validated amorphous models (\claim{C037}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Required validation}
\label{sec:mono-concl-validation}

\paragraph{Measurements (by information per cost).}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Off-state floor against drain bias on the existing chips (strip $\times7$ against diffusion $\times1.02$ between 0.1
and 0.7~V), with the gate current recorded.
\item Device statistics: at least ten devices per thickness from one fabrication run, with dual sweeps and recorded sweep
history.
\item Thickness metrology of the same films (XRR, TEM) and the stack order.
\item Capacitance--voltage flat band, optical gap and Hall density against thickness, for \InO{} and IWO.
\item A dense thickness series (about 1~nm steps, 3--15~nm) at 0, 2 and 4\,\% W, capped and uncapped.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Calculations.} A dipole-corrected SCF of the 0.695~nm film; a lower-density and a hybrid-functional W cell;
charged vacancy cells with corrections; band runs of the W films; and, for the amorphous question, a re-registered
series with at least two independently quenched seeds per endpoint relaxed in one smearing family. Costs are in
\cref{tab:mono-mass-runs}.

\paragraph{Governance.} The meeting with the fabrication group on permission, attribution, AI processing and authorship;
an external timestamp for the frozen registrations; adoption of the charter; the release decision for the sealed
amorphous results; and completion of the device campaign under its registered rules.

\section{Outlook}
\label{sec:mono-concl-outlook}

The strongest original result available today is computational and crystalline: a confinement series of \InO{} films
whose mass increase follows from the bulk band structure, with an explicit account of what transfers to a device model
and what does not. The central dimensional question about W in amorphous films is protocol-ready but, by the frozen
rules, cannot be answered as registered by the present campaign. A manuscript framework for \emph{npj Computational
Materials} that states these limits is in \file{research_monograph/manuscript_npj/}; it contains placeholders for every
sealed result and makes no claim that the evidence does not support.

\begin{keyresult}
Demonstrated: the device metrics, the failure of every 6.3~nm prediction, the crystalline confinement series with masses
explained by bulk non-parabolicity, the resonant W level at high filling (deep-donor reading withdrawn), the failed
ML force check, and the impossibility of a registered H-N crossover verdict. Strongly supported: contact resistance
cannot explain the trend; the floor is not gate leakage; the published mobilities are saturation-formula numbers; the
6.3~nm failures follow from a flat threshold where confinement predicts 0.2~V. Everything about amorphous W and the shared
model's transfer is sealed. The decisive next steps are measurements: the floor's drain-bias dependence, device
statistics and thickness metrology.
\end{keyresult}
