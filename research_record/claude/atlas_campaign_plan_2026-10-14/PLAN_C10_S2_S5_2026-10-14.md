### 1.3 The S2-11 failure in plain words

**What failed.** S2-11 refined the oxide y-mesh at 2.0 nm with the worst-case oxide set (`oxide_mesh_factor` 0.5; in interval count Al2O3 about ×1.5 and HfO2 about ×2, with the y = 0 line kept; scorer `refinement` field). Under that change the resolved gate current moved by up to 2.86 decades:
- 73 of 75 resolved points moved by more than 0.1 dec;
- at V_G = −3 V the current moved by 0.315 dec.

These are COMPUTED (ATLAS) values from `hc_mesh_run_0076_run_0067.json`. The registered mesh-stability prediction therefore FAILED. It stays recorded, and no later pass can erase it (Codex, 03:36Z).

**Numerical quality of the two curves** (CALCULATED from the COMPUTED (ATLAS) points of the same file; quality statements only, no physical reading):
- |I_G(V_G)| is not monotonic for V_G ≤ −0.5 V: 1 inverted step on the ×1 mesh and 6 on the ×0.5 mesh. The 2.86 dec maximum is an isolated spike at −0.70 V on the ×0.5 mesh (9.43e-20 A/µm against neighbours of 2.02e-21 and 2.69e-22 A/µm).
- 42 of the 75 resolved points lie below the solver's absolute current tolerance `ir.tol=1e-19` (`solver.yaml` l.4; unit A/µm in 2-D ASSUMED) on at least one mesh.
- **The FAIL is robust to both:** all 33 points with |I_G| ≥ 1e-19 A/µm on both meshes change by more than 0.1 dec (maximum 1.65 dec at −1.4 V).
- Non-monotonic steps point to nonlinear-solver error as well as discretisation (manual 5.28 l.13637–13642 on poor QTNLSC convergence). The mesh branch therefore includes a tolerance replay (§7).

**What this means for H-C.**
- Every QTNLSC-based H-C verdict stays PROVISIONAL or UNRESOLVED.
- No floor-scale current from the worst-case set may be interpreted (S2 stop rule: "interpret it only after S2-11 … agrees within 0.1 dec in log10 I_G", `campaign_C10_S2_offfloor.json`; design l.305). The plan therefore makes **no** statement on worst-set magnitude or flatness in the floor window. The values sit in the scored file and stay uninterpreted.

**What the plan does about it** (§2.5, §7):
- The nominal-set null rests on the CALCULATED analytic bounds. Their margin of at least 9 decades is conditional on the Z9 recomputation with the ALD Al2O3 and Monaghan HfO2 parameters. QTNLSC is reported beside them as PROVISIONAL.
- The leakage question is cross-checked with FNORD, which ATLAS evaluates from the interface field. That field is exact in a charge-free planar oxide, so FNORD is *argued* to be mesh-robust (ASSUMED; no FNORD mesh check is planned).
- The ITAT result is made conditional on the slice-point check that the manual itself requires.
- A bounded tunnelling-mesh branch runs: automatic-slice refinement or a tolerance replay, plus the user's QTX/QTY.MESH series as a magnitude cross-check only. Its pre-registered claims are limited to 2.0 nm with the worst-case set.

**Why the mesh branch is decision-relevant** (without interpreting any worst-set magnitude):
- No QTNLSC I_G magnitude can be quoted anywhere (P1, the paper's I_G statements) until a convergence test passes.
- The A41(d) OXM screens (Block C) can only be read if oxide-mesh convergence is reachable at all on this build.
- If convergence is infeasible on the 32-bit build, that is itself a reportable limitation, and the 13.2 nm leakage statement must rest on FNORD and the CALCULATED bounds.

## 7. Tunnelling-mesh convergence branch (user request; PA-4)

### 7.1 Manual basis

- **Stepwise potential.** QTNLSC computes T(E) by a transfer matrix on a stepwise potential, so the manual recommends a fine mesh along the tunnelling direction (5.28 l.13505–13507). The 2004 manual asks for "at least several mesh points" (l.8700–8703).
- **Slicing.** On an Atlas-built mesh the slicing is automatic: one slice per semiconductor–oxide segment, or SHAPEOX as the alternative (l.13660–13677).
- **QT mesh.** `QTX.MESH`/`QTY.MESH` are the stated option for Athena/DevEdit meshes. The QT mesh must enclose the insulator without overextending, and it is interpolated to the device mesh (l.13665–13670; statement l.61739–61765).
- **No point-count control.** QTNLSC has no slice-count or point-count parameter (`04` §4.1). The only controls are the oxide y-mesh, the x-mesh along y = 0, SHAPEOX and the QT mesh. The 0.1 dec criterion is the register's (ASSUMED).

### 7.2 Why the registered mesh is not expected to be converged (CALCULATED)

- **Baseline step against decay length.** The largest HfO2 step is 1.5–3.4 × 1/κ on the baseline mesh and 0.7–1.7 × 1/κ after the S2-11 refinement (HfO2 intervals about ×2) (`04` §4.4).
- **Two regimes in S2-11** (COMPUTED (ATLAS); mesh-to-mesh changes only, no magnitude reading):
  - For V_G ≥ −0.45 V the change is systematic and always the same sign: −0.17 to −0.44 dec, median −0.35 (54 points).
  - For V_G ≤ −0.5 V the change is erratic (−0.49 to +2.86 dec), with inverted |I_G| steps (1 on ×1, 6 on ×0.5) and many points below `ir.tol` (§1.3). That pattern is not a clean discretisation signal.
- **What a further refinement would do** (ASSUMED error orders): about 0.2 dec if the error is first-order, about 0.1 dec if it is second-order. Z13 replaces this guess with an offline prediction.
