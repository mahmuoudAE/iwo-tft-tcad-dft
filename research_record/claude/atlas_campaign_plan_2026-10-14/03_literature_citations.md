# 03 - Primary-literature check of every LITERATURE / ASSUMED value in the C10 campaign (2026-10-14)

Scope: values in `IWO_PHYSICS_CONSTRAINED_MODEL_V1/config/iwo_material_model_v3.yaml`, `config/physics_options.yaml` and
`analysis_2026-10-10_campaign/CAMPAIGN_DESIGN.md` sections 4.4-4.5. Read-only audit: nothing registered was edited. Every
"proposed action" below is a candidate for a logged amendment or an S6 sensitivity, to be checked by Codex and manually
(TCAD check rule) before use.

Labels: MEASURED, COMPUTED (our DFT), LITERATURE (citation + DOI), CALCULATED (formula + inputs shown), FITTED, ASSUMED.
"Verified" = bibliographic data checked against Crossref or the publisher/repository record in this session. A value marked
UNVERIFIED was not read in the source itself in this session (abstract-only access, paywall, or memory).

Verdicts: SUPPORTED (source gives our value for our material/phase), IN RANGE (inside the published spread, or right number
for a proxy material), NOT SUPPORTED (source contradicts the value or its attribution).

Sources opened in full text in this session: Fan et al. 2021 (PMC full text), Fung et al. 2009 (PDF, Table I), Robertson and
Wallace 2015 (accepted manuscript, Tables 1 and 3, section 5.2), Januar et al. 2026, Wang et al. 2022, Lin et al. 2022, Si et
al. 2021 and Kim et al. 2024 (local text copies in `inputs/papers_text/`), ATLAS 5.28 manual text
(`report_latex_full/tools/atlas_manual_pages.txt`). All other entries rest on abstracts or bibliographic records, as marked.

## H. Tunnelling-mesh branch: what the literature can and cannot settle

- The QTNLSC numerics (transfer-matrix T(E) on a stepwise potential; quasi-1-D slices; automatic slicing for an Atlas-built
  mesh; QTX/QTY.MESH for Athena/DevEdit meshes, enclosing the insulator) are documented only in the ATLAS manual. Authority:
  5.28 text l.13505-13507 and l.13660-13676; second reference: 2004 manual l.8730-8757. No journal paper replaces the
  convergence study.
- S2-11 (oxide y-mesh x0.5) moved I_G by up to 2.86 dec. That is larger than every parameter-level change above. The
  Monaghan mass/affinity pair (C6) and the ALD Al2O3 affinity (C3) each change the tunnelling exponent by O(1) in the
  direct-tunnelling limit. **Order of work: mesh convergence (automatic-slice refinement series, with the QT-mesh series as
  a magnitude cross-check only) before any literature-parameter sensitivity on I_G.** A parameter sensitivity run on an
  unconverged mesh cannot be interpreted.
- The FN (FNORD) path is mesh-insensitive in its constants (C10). Under the Monaghan pair the FN exponent B is unchanged
  within 1 % (C6). FNORD is therefore the cheap cross-check for the field-emission regime while QTNLSC converges.
